Otoscope Clinic

Precise ear examination and diagnosis for infections, wax build-up, hearing changes and everyday ear discomfort.

Description

The Otoscope Clinic uses high-precision otoscopy to examine the outer ear canal and eardrum, helping our specialists accurately diagnose infections, blockages, injuries and structural issues without discomfort.

Procedure

A gentle, non-invasive visual examination is performed using an illuminated otoscope. If required, microscopic ear examination and safe ear cleaning (wax removal) are carried out in the same visit.

Advantages

Painless & quick Accurate diagnosis Safe for all ages Same-day results

Who Needs This

  • Persistent ear pain or itching
  • Ear discharge or unusual odour
  • Sudden or gradual hearing loss
  • Feeling of blocked or full ears

Key Benefits

  • Early detection prevents complications
  • Immediate treatment plan on the same visit
Book Otoscope Consultation

Endoscope Clinic

Advanced nasal and throat endoscopy for accurate, minimally-invasive diagnosis of deeper ENT conditions.

Description

Endoscopy allows our specialists to directly visualise the nasal passages, sinuses, throat and voice box using a slim, flexible scope — revealing conditions that a standard examination cannot detect.

Procedure

A thin endoscope with a light and camera is gently passed through the nose or mouth under local anaesthesia (if needed), with real-time images guiding immediate diagnosis.

Advantages

Minimally invasive Real-time visualization High diagnostic accuracy Outpatient procedure

Who Needs This

  • Chronic nasal blockage or sinusitis
  • Persistent throat discomfort or hoarseness
  • Recurrent nosebleeds
  • Suspected nasal polyps or growths

Key Benefits

  • Detects issues invisible to standard exams
  • Guides precise, targeted treatment
Book Endoscopy Consultation

Vertigo Clinic

Specialized assessment and management of dizziness, spinning sensations and balance disorders.

Description

Vertigo can stem from inner-ear disorders, nerve issues or other causes. Our Vertigo Clinic combines detailed history, balance testing and ENT expertise to identify the root cause accurately.

Procedure

Assessment includes a detailed symptom history, positional balance tests, and ear examination, followed by a tailored treatment plan — from repositioning manoeuvres to medication and therapy.

Advantages

Root-cause diagnosis Non-surgical treatment options Rapid symptom relief Personalized care plan

Who Needs This

  • Sudden spinning or dizziness episodes
  • Loss of balance while walking
  • Nausea triggered by head movement
  • Ringing in the ears with dizziness

Key Benefits

  • Restores confidence in daily movement
  • Reduces fall risk, especially in seniors
Book Vertigo Consultation

Dysphagia Clinic (Swallowing Disorders)

Expert evaluation and therapy for swallowing difficulties, ensuring safe and comfortable eating.

Description

Dysphagia — difficulty swallowing — can affect eating, drinking and quality of life. Our clinic assesses the throat and swallowing mechanism to identify causes and design a safe recovery plan.

Procedure

Evaluation includes clinical swallow assessment and, where needed, endoscopic examination of the throat, followed by personalised swallowing therapy and dietary guidance.

Advantages

Reduces choking risk Personalized therapy Improves nutrition intake Multidisciplinary approach

Who Needs This

  • Difficulty or pain while swallowing
  • Frequent coughing or choking during meals
  • Sensation of food stuck in the throat
  • Unexplained weight loss related to eating

Key Benefits

  • Safer, more comfortable eating
  • Prevents aspiration-related complications
Book Dysphagia Consultation

Voice Clinic

Diagnosis and therapy for voice disorders affecting speech clarity, vocal strength and quality.

Description

Our Voice Clinic evaluates the vocal cords and larynx to diagnose hoarseness, vocal strain, nodules and other voice disorders, common among teachers, singers and professionals who rely on their voice.

Procedure

Assessment includes a detailed voice history and endoscopic examination of the vocal cords, followed by voice therapy techniques and, if necessary, medical or surgical intervention.

Advantages

Restores vocal clarity Non-invasive therapy first Ideal for voice professionals Long-term vocal health

Who Needs This

  • Persistent hoarseness or voice loss
  • Vocal fatigue after speaking or singing
  • Teachers, singers & public speakers
  • Noticeable change in voice pitch

Key Benefits

  • Protects long-term vocal health
  • Improves confidence in communication
Book Voice Consultation

Hearing Assessment Desirable Audiology

Comprehensive hearing tests to accurately measure and understand your hearing health.

Description

Our in-house Decibel Audiology Centre performs complete audiometric testing to measure hearing thresholds across frequencies, identifying the type and degree of hearing loss, if any.

Procedure

A qualified audiologist conducts pure-tone audiometry and related tests in a calibrated sound-treated environment, generating a detailed hearing profile within the same visit.

Advantages

Painless & fast Precise hearing profile In-house, no referrals needed Suitable for all ages

Who Needs This

  • Difficulty following conversations
  • Frequently increasing TV/phone volume
  • Children with delayed speech development
  • Routine hearing checks for seniors

Key Benefits

  • Early detection of hearing loss
  • Guides the right hearing aid solution
Book Hearing Assessment

Hearing Aid Trial Desirable Audiology

Try before you decide — risk-free hearing aid trials matched to your lifestyle and hearing profile.

Description

Choosing the right hearing aid is personal. We offer supervised trial fittings of leading hearing aid brands so you can experience clarity in real-world settings before making a decision.

Procedure

Based on your hearing assessment, our audiologist programs a suitable device, fits it, and guides you through a trial period with follow-up fine-tuning for optimal comfort and clarity.

Advantages

Risk-free trial period Personalized programming Multiple brand options Expert fine-tuning

Who Needs This

  • Diagnosed with hearing loss
  • First-time hearing aid users
  • Considering an upgrade from an older device

Key Benefits

  • Confidence before purchase
  • Perfectly matched to your hearing needs
Book a Hearing Aid Trial

Hearing Aid Service & Repairs Desirable Audiology

Keep your hearing aid performing at its best with expert servicing, cleaning and repairs.

Description

Hearing aids need regular maintenance to work reliably. Our audiology team offers cleaning, troubleshooting, reprogramming and repair services to extend your device's life and performance.

Procedure

Devices are inspected for wear, moisture damage or malfunction, cleaned thoroughly, and either repaired on-site or sent for specialised repair, with a replacement unit offered where possible.

Advantages

Extends device lifespan Prevents sound quality loss Quick turnaround Genuine parts & expertise

Who Needs This

  • Reduced or distorted hearing aid sound
  • Device not switching on or holding charge
  • Routine annual maintenance

Key Benefits

  • Uninterrupted hearing aid performance
  • Avoids costly premature replacement
Book Service & Repair

Home Care Desirable Audiology

Audiology support brought to your doorstep for elderly or mobility-limited patients.

Description

For patients unable to visit the clinic easily, our Home Care service brings hearing check-ups, hearing aid adjustments and basic servicing directly to your home.

Procedure

A scheduled home visit is arranged by our audiology team, carrying portable equipment to assess hearing aid function, perform cleaning, and address minor issues on the spot.

Advantages

Convenient scheduling Ideal for elderly patients No travel required Personal, unhurried care

Who Needs This

  • Elderly patients with limited mobility
  • Post-surgical or bedridden patients
  • Families seeking routine hearing aid checks

Key Benefits

  • Care without leaving home
  • Peace of mind for families
Request Home Care Visit

Speech Therapy Desirable Audiology

Structured therapy to improve speech clarity, articulation and communication confidence.

Description

Speech therapy supports both children with developmental speech delays and adults recovering speech clarity after hearing loss, surgery or neurological conditions.

Procedure

Our speech therapist conducts an initial communication assessment, then designs structured sessions using proven articulation, language and auditory training techniques.

Advantages

Improves communication Tailored for children & adults Works alongside hearing aids Progress-tracked sessions

Who Needs This

  • Children with delayed speech development
  • Adults with unclear articulation
  • Patients adjusting to new hearing aids

Key Benefits

  • Builds confident, clear communication
  • Supports better academic & social outcomes
