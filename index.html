About Our Clinic

A Legacy of Trust in Ear, Nose & Throat Care

Vasu ENT Surgical Clinic has been serving the people of Puducherry since 1988 — the first to establish a dedicated ENT surgical practice in the region. Over three decades, we have combined surgical expertise with compassionate, accessible care.

  • Pioneer ENT surgical practice, operating in nearly every nursing home in Puducherry.
  • Over-the-phone & online appointment systems built for genuine patient convenience.
  • In-house Audiology and Pharmacy services, all under a single roof.
