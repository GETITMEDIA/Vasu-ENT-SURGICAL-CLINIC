Endoscope Clinic

Advanced diagnostic procedures to examine nasal passages, sinuses, throat and vocal cords using high-definition cameras.

Comfortable & quick HD digital capture Accurate diagnosis

Overview

Our Endoscope Clinic utilizes specialized thin, flexible and rigid endoscopes equipped with high-definition digital cameras to look inside nasal airways and the deep throat, providing immediate clarity on complex ENT symptoms.

How It Works

A mild local anesthetic spray is used to ensure a completely comfortable experience. The endoscope is gently introduced, and real-time high-definition video is viewed on a monitor and recorded for your records.

Advantages

Minimally invasive & safe
High-definition visual capture
Same-day diagnostic report
Guides precise treatment

Who Needs This

  • Chronic sinusitis or nasal blockages
  • Recurrent nosebleeds (epistaxis)
  • Voice change or persistent hoarseness
  • Feeling of lump in the throat

Key Benefits

  • Pinpoint diagnosis of deep tissues
  • Immediate visual confirmation for the patient
