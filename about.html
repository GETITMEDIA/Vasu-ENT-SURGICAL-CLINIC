Meet Our Founder

Dr. L. Vasudevan

Founder & Chief ENT Surgeon, Vasu ENT Surgical Clinic

With over 30 years of dedicated practice, Dr. Vasudevan established Vasu ENT Surgical Clinic in 1988 as the very first dedicated ENT surgical practice in Puducherry. His career spans surgical work in nearly every major nursing home across the region, and he continues to lead a clinic built on precision diagnosis, surgical expertise, and genuine patient care.

Under his guidance, the clinic pioneered accessible healthcare in the region — introducing an over-the-phone appointment system two decades ago, long before it became common practice, and building an in-house audiology and pharmacy ecosystem so patients receive complete care under one roof.

Our Journey

Clinic History & Milestones

Three decades of growth, trust and specialty expansion.

1988

Clinic Founded

Vasu ENT Surgical Clinic opens on Bussy Street, Puducherry — the region's first dedicated ENT surgical practice.

1990s

City-Wide Surgical Practice

Our surgeon begins operating across nearly every major nursing home in Puducherry, building trust throughout the community.

2000s

Phone Appointment System Launched

Vasu ENT becomes the only clinic in the region offering an over-the-phone appointment system, well ahead of its time.

2010s

In-House Audiology & Pharmacy

Introduction of in-house Decibel Audiology Centre and pharmacy, enabling complete ear-care under a single roof.

2020s

Digital & Computerized Systems

Fully computerized appointment records and an online booking system launched for a modern patient experience.

Vasu ENT Surgical Clinic continues to serve Puducherry with dedicated Otoscope, Endoscope, Vertigo, Voice, Dysphagia and Audiology clinics.
